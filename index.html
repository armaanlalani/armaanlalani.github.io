    I started working part time at Overbond during the final two months of my undergraduate degree and transitioned to a full-time role in May 2023. As a Data Scientist, I was involved in two major projects: Smart Order Routing (SOR) and Layer2 Bond Pricing.

    Smart Order Routing

    I was the lead owner and developer of SOR, a new product in Overbond's suite. The goal of SOR is to provide algorithmic and aggregated fixed income trading routes in order to minimize transaction costs. Fixed income trading typically involves four major venues: Over the Counter (OTC), Request for Quote (RFQ), All to All, and Direct Channels. Traders usually have to check several venues and channels to find the best path to execution, which is the problem SOR aims to solve.

    OTC and RFQ prices are predicted using trained machine learning models, and associated quantities are estimated using dealer inventory and market capacity analytics built on Transaction Cost Analysis (TCA). After gathering prices, venues, and available dealers, a linear program solves for the best path to execution across the available sources.

    Layer2 Bond Pricing

    I was also a developer for Overbond's Layer2 Bond Pricing Model. The goal of Layer2 is to provide live RFQ pricing analytics so clients can minimize transaction costs while also winning the RFQ auction. The model is a boosting algorithm that uses decision trees to predict bond prices from bond-level, issuer-level, and sector-level analytics.

    I was responsible for conducting backtests for clients, which involved training individual models on each client's historical RFQ data. I owned every stage of the data pipeline: exploratory data analysis, data cleansing, model training, model output analysis, and presentation of results.

    Center for Management of Technology and Entrepreneurship

    Fourth-year thesis comparing handcrafted and deep feature-based methods for detecting synthetic speech at a major financial institution.

    • Deep Learning

    My fourth-year thesis was completed through U of T's Center for Management of Technology and Entrepreneurship with a financial institution (The Bank), which cannot be disclosed due to an NDA.

    The rise of deepfake technology in recent years has underscored the importance of distinguishing between authentic and synthetic content. For established financial institutions such as The Bank, ensuring security and confidentiality for clients and themselves is a top priority. However, voiceprint technology, which relies on vocal features to authenticate clients over the phone, is vulnerable to synthesized speech. The Bank recognizes the potential risks posed by synthetic speech and the need to identify effective mitigation techniques.

    To this end, the study compares the performance of handcrafted and deep feature-based methods of synthetic speech detection across several key metrics: accuracy, equal error rate, processing time, and trainable parameters. Two models were developed and tested, and the results indicate that the deep features model outperforms the handcrafted features model on three of the four metrics, suggesting that it should be prioritized for implementation.

  6. Machine Learning Consultant

    Perimeter Medical Imaging AI

    September 2022 – December 2022

    Engineering Capstone using deep learning to denoise OCT images and shorten margin assessments during breast-conserving surgery.

    • Deep Learning
    • Medical Imaging
    • Denoising
    Read moreRead less

    My fourth-year Engineering Capstone project was completed in collaboration with Perimeter Medical Imaging, an AI company that uses deep learning models to help surgeons determine whether cancerous tissue was successfully removed during breast-conserving surgery (BCS).

    The primary problem presented by Perimeter was the time required to conduct a margin assessment, the process used to determine from OCT images whether the surgeon successfully removed the tumor. An accurate margin assessment requires denoised OCT images, which were produced by combining two signals to reduce noise, taking approximately two to three minutes.

    This timeframe was problematic for two reasons: it reduces the number of patients a surgeon is able to see, and it can keep patients under unnecessary sedation if the assessment reveals no malignant tissue. Our solution used a deep learning model to reduce the noise in individual signals, reducing the total time needed to conduct a margin assessment.

    I had the tremendous opportunity to work at OPTrust during my Professional Experience Year (PEY) as a CMG Investment Co-op on the TFCPS team. My role and team were heavily focused on making strong data-driven investment decisions using a variety of techniques from data science and machine learning.

    Equity regime detection

    The model consisted of two parts: first, using L1 trend filtering to numerically identify historical periods of growth and decline in equity, and second, training a random forest on several macroeconomic indicators to predict regime switches. The model performed well out of sample and was able to predict the regime switches during the Global Financial Crisis and COVID. It continues to be used in production for Severe Market Decline and Systematic Risk Allocation.

    Risk mitigant bootstrapping

    This model tests the effectiveness of various risk-mitigating strategies against the S&P. It samples historical returns to create a simulated path environment over a specified period, then introduces a risk mitigant (gold, bonds, currencies, etc.) or a strategy (trend, options, etc.) to attempt to reduce the VaR95 of the original portfolio by a target percentage. The investment division now uses it to evaluate strategies.

    Futures roll prediction

    I built a convolutional neural network to predict price movements of futures rolls and created the entire data pipeline: extracting the appropriate roll tickers based on the roll schedule, querying Bloomberg for price, volume, open interest, and Commitment of Traders data, and preprocessing the data for the model. The model did not perform as well as expected, but the project taught me a lot about rolling futures and the capabilities of the Bloomberg Terminal.

    Reporting

    I also worked on a number of reporting projects, including daily reports on FX exposure and P&L, analysis of model slippage for Risk Reduced Equities using intraday data, and reporting emails for various fund portfolios.
